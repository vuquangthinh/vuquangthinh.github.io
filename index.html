AmbuSun | RCM Services

AmbuSun is a healthcare revenue cycle management platform focused on helping providers streamline claims, denials, payer follow-up, reimbursement workflows and revenue visibility. It brings together operational reporting, claim management and AI-assisted support for billing teams.

Overview

AmbuSun helps healthcare organizations improve financial performance by reducing claim delays, managing denial lifecycles and accelerating reimbursement cycles. The platform supports end-to-end revenue operations from patient access and billing through collections and payer follow-up.

My role

I worked as a Fullstack developer building and evolving the reporting, denial management and AI Chat modules. My responsibilities included product workflows, backend APIs, data models, dashboard logic, UI implementation, and operational features used by revenue cycle teams.

What I built

  • Denial management workflows for tracking, prioritizing and resolving claim denials.
  • Reporting modules for KPI dashboards, aging buckets, payer performance and operational visibility.
  • AI Chat features to help users quickly search claim data, summarize denial cases and support agent workflows.
  • Admin and operational tools for status tracking, case ownership, escalation and audit trails.
  • Data integrations and analytics layers to support decision-making across claims, payment and collections.

Mô tả ngắn gọn

AmbuSun là nền tảng quản lý doanh thu y tế (RCM) giúp các cơ sở chăm sóc sức khỏe tối ưu hóa quy trình claim, denial, theo dõi nhà cung cấp thanh toán và tăng hiệu quả thu hồi doanh thu. Nền tảng kết hợp báo cáo vận hành, quản lý denial và AI Chat để hỗ trợ đội ngũ billing, revenue cycle và support.

Tôi làm Fullstack developer, xây dựng và phát triển các module reporting và quản lý denial, đồng thời tích hợp AI Chat hỗ trợ xử lý câu hỏi, tóm tắt hồ sơ denial và hỗ trợ nhân viên vận hành làm việc hiệu quả hơn.
